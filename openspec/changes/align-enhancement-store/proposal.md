# Proposal

## Why

The enhancements store replaces the `enhancements/NNNN/` layout. A decision number (`NNNN:Dn`, `NNNN Dn`) no longer names anything a reader can open, because the new entries have `requirements`, not numbered decisions. The workspace rule is now: cite an enhancement as its id beside a reason in words, never a decision number and never a lone id. docs-kit still cites the old form in its rules, comments, contracts and specs, and its `link` citation policy still links `NNNN:Dn` to a `decisions` page that new-layout entries do not have.

## What Changes

- `openspec/config.yaml` and `AGENTS.md`: add `references:` to the store `opm-enhancements`, the `Delivers` proposal rule, the comment rule and the archive guidance; drop the `NNNN:Dn` form.
- Rewrite every enhancement citation in tracked docs, live specs, archived changes and Go comments and strings to "enhancement NNNN" beside its reason. The first pass is `enh cite-rewrite`; a hand pass clears its worklist and redoes every edit the tool made in text that describes the old forms.
- The `link` citation policy (`internal/doctext/policy.go`) keeps only the link from "enhancement NNNN" to `/enhancements/NNNN/`. A decision citation is removed under `link`, as under `strip`.
- The two enforcement rows of the cue-catalog extractor link `[enhancement 0010](/enhancements/0010/)` instead of a decision page.
- `docs/contracts.md` (Doc-comment rules, C6, C8, C18) and the live specs state the new `link` policy.
- Code that strips or reads old citation forms stays, with its tests and fixtures: a docs revision builds an old release tag whose comments still hold them. The lint and the rewrite skip these exact paths:
  - `internal/doctext/doctext_test.go`, `internal/doctext/policy_test.go`: tests of the strip rules, old forms in strings;
  - `internal/render/testdata/library/`, `internal/render/testdata/golden/library/`: a snapshot of library source with old comments, and its golden pages;
  - `internal/extract/cuecatalog/testdata/catalog/`, `internal/extract/cuedefs/testdata/defs/`, `internal/extract/goapi/testdata/mod/`, `internal/extract/crd/testdata/tree/`: fixtures that keep one old-form citation each, so the strip tests prove removal;
  - `internal/render/testdata/operator/`: a copy of opm-operator CRDs and its docs page (operator output, regenerated by the operator);
  - `internal/dialect/testdata/`, `internal/extract/enhancements/testdata/`, `internal/extract/enhancements/extract_test.go`, `internal/render/section_target_test.go`, `internal/render/testdata/golden/enhancements/`: fixtures and tests of the old entry layout (synthetic entry 0025 with its `decisions` page).
- The goldens under `internal/render/testdata/golden/` and the `*.golden.json` files are regenerated by `-update`, never edited by hand. The fixture catalog digest in `internal/build/digest_test.go` moves with the enforcement rows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `crd-extractor`: "The crd page is one completable page" (the scenario "Citation linked" shows an enhancement reference, and a scenario shows a removed decision citation) and "CRD text never reaches the page as markup" (the renderer links enhancement references only).
- `enhancements-bundle`: "The link policy links enhancement references" (a decision citation is removed, not linked).
- `opm-docs-cli`: "docs-kit.cue configures the bundles a repository builds" (the scenario "Citations linked" shows an enhancement link).

Every scenario name is kept. The live specs already hold the new text; the deltas record the change.

## Impact

- Consumers of a contract: `opm-operator` (`docs-kit.cue` uses `citations: "link"`; its own change drops that line, per the enhancements design). `core`, `cli`, `library`, `opm` and `opmodel.dev` use the default `strip` policy; their generated pages change only where their rewritten comments now say "enhancement NNNN".
- `catalog_opm` pages change under every policy: the two enforcement rows (a provider-fulfilled contract, a trait without a handler) link to enhancement 0010. The catalog bundle digest of the fixture moves.
- Release class: the PR is titled `feat(doctext)`, so release-please makes a minor release. The commits are `docs`, `test` and `chore`; the title carries the release.

## Enhancement

None. The store entry for this alignment lives in `enhancements`; this repo holds no line of its `tasks.md`.
